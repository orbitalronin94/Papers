# PART I — THE AMBITION

## §1. The starting point

PUSFRE began with a wager. The master equation has already been written above. Its terms are:

- $\Phi_i$ — capacity or geometry of agent $i$
- $\Psi_i$ — consistency or "debt" of agent $i$
- $\Omega_i$ — invocation or usage frequency
- $\alpha$ — competition exponent
- $\varepsilon_i$ — stochastic noise

The corpus was presented as a set of 19 documents: reductions of 288 classical theorems, 58 theorems on routing fatigue, 74 demonstrated theorems, 45 prospective crises, a programming language (RONIN) with a Python runtime and a compiler specified in Rust.

This is extravagant. And deliberately so.

## §2. The structure of the original corpus

The corpus is organized in levels: foundational papers (Geometry of Forgetting, Agent Ecology, Ontological Debt), unification (Unified Dynamics), foundations (Fundamental Theorem), operational manual, extensions (Computational Extension, Routing Fatigue), self-review, Atlas of Reductions, RONIN language specification, and a final Easter egg.

Three characteristics distinguish it from a conventional paper: it is cumulative, self-referential, and totalizing. No domain is excluded.

## §3. The five axioms of the Fundamental Theorem

The heart of the corpus is the proof that the Master Equation is the only fitness function satisfying five axioms: monotonicity in $\Phi$, inconsistency penalty in $\Psi$, decreasing competition in $\Omega$, multiplicative separability, and rescaling invariance.

The proof proceeds by derivation and concludes with:

$$F = C \cdot \Phi \cdot (1 - \gamma \Psi) \cdot \Omega^\alpha \cdot \varepsilon$$

The elegance is real. So is its fragility: Axiom IV (multiplicative separability) is the strongest, and the corpus itself acknowledges this in its self-review.

## §4. The expansion

Between June and August 2026, the corpus expands: Atlas of Reductions with 288 classical theorems, Treatise on Routing Fatigue with 58 theorems, Treatise on Infinite Reduction, RONIN 1.0, Unified Treatise on Cyberwarfare with 100 exploits and 100 defences.

Ambition is at its peak. And the corpus explicitly embraces it in its README:

> *"The RONIN Corpus is a formal research programme aspiring to a general theory of finite systems with scarce resources."*

# PART IV — THE METHOD

## §13. Definition

**Definition 13.1 (RONIN structural-diagnosis method).** Let $M$ be a nonlinear model with parameters $\theta \in \Theta \subseteq \mathbb{R}^p$, data $D$, noise $\sigma$, and a question $Q$ about the model. The method is a seven-phase procedure for determining whether $\theta$ is identifiable given $(M, D, \sigma, Q)$, before attempting a fit.

The seven phases are:

1. **Categorical declaration** — classify the model and question as Category A/B/C/D.
2. **Pre-fit diagnosis** — calculate the FIM at typical values $\theta_{\text{typ}}$.
3. **Spectral decomposition** — perform an SVD of the FIM.
4. **Regime classification** — use calibrated thresholds.
5. **Identification of invariants** — find an identifiable $I(\theta)$.
6. **Domain delimitation** — declare the range of $\Omega$ and the validity conditions.
7. **Reporting with a confusion matrix** — publish TP/FP/TN/FN for the mechanism.

## §14. Phase 1: Categorical declaration

Before any analysis, declare: Category A (analytically demonstrated), Category B (reasonable inference), Category C (operational hypothesis), Category D (heuristic analogy).

**Rule.** Every claim that is not Category A must state which Category A result it derives from, which additional assumptions it requires, and what empirical evidence supports it.

## §15. Phase 2: Pre-fit diagnosis (FIM)

Calculate the Fisher information matrix:

$$\mathcal{I}(\theta) = \frac{1}{\sigma^2} \sum_{i=1}^{N} \nabla_\theta C(t_i; \theta) \cdot \nabla_\theta C(t_i; \theta)^\top$$

evaluated at typical values $\theta_{\text{typ}}$. Caveat: the FIM is local. It requires documented $\theta_{\text{typ}}$.

## §16. Phase 3: Spectral decomposition (SVD)

Calculate the SVD of the FIM: $\mathcal{I} = U \Sigma V^\top$. The last column $v_p$ of $V$ corresponds to the least well-determined direction. Parameters with the largest contribution $|v_p[j]| > 0.3$ are problematic.

## §17. Phase 4: Regime classification

Calculate the condition number $\kappa(\mathcal{I}) = \lambda_{\max}/\lambda_{\min}$ and classify:

| Condition number | Regime | Action |
|---------------------|---------|--------|
| $< 10^3$ | Identifiable | Report all parameters with CIs |
| $10^3$ – $10^6$ | Marginal | Report with an explicit warning |
| $\geq 10^6$ | Non-identifiable | Fix, reparameterize, or collect more data |

## §18. Phase 5: Identification of invariants

Given an identified degeneracy, find the invariant $I(\theta)$ that is constant on each fibre and separates distinct fibres.

Hill example: $I(K, \alpha) = (K^{-\alpha}, \alpha)$.

PBPK example: $I(\text{Vt}, \text{Kp}) = \text{Vt} \cdot \text{Kp}$.

## §19. Phase 6: Domain delimitation

Explicitly declare: the range of $\Omega$ in orders of magnitude, model structure, visibility of saturation, dependence on $\theta_{\text{typ}}$, and validity conditions.

## §20. Phase 7: Reporting with a confusion matrix

For each domain: PASS/PASS (TP), PASS/FAIL (FP), FAIL/FAIL (TN), FAIL/PASS (FN). Calculate precision and recall.

## §21. The meta-method: four principles

**Principle 1: Diagnose before fitting.**

**Principle 2: Categorize before claiming.**

**Principle 3: Delimit before generalizing.**

**Principle 4: Preregister before testing.**
